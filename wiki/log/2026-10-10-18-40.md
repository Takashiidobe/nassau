# Implement CharVector over strings

_2026-10-10 18:40:07_

Audited VECTOR, VECTOR_SLICE, MONO_VECTOR, MONO_VECTOR_SLICE, and the character-vector instances. Added CharVector using its specified String.string representation, plus CharVectorSlice using its specified Substring.substring representation. Generic immutable vectors and Word8 vectors still need runtime support in nassau-949.13 and nassau-949.14; both are dependencies of nassau-617.3. No fake list-backed vectors or new runtime feature was introduced.
