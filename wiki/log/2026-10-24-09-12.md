# Compile word arithmetic and comparisons

_2026-10-24 09:12:14_

Closed nassau-949.12. Six of its seven fixtures already compiled; the last, types/valid-overloading.sml, was blocked on word + - * div mod and < <= > >=. Added Prim::WordAdd/Sub/Mul/Div/Mod/Lt/Le/Gt/Ge. Words share the tagged-int representation (63-bit payload): add/sub/mul wrap with no overflow check, div/mod are unsigned and raise Div on zero, comparisons are unsigned. lower.rs picks the word prims when the operand type is word, including the infix mod. Removed the RUNTIME-SKIP and CHECK-RUN-ERR from valid-overloading. Added runtime/word-arithmetic.sml (exit code check against Poly/ML) and graduated conformance.unsupported/static/word-arithmetic.sml to conformance/dynamic. The rest of the Word family (bitwise, shifts, conversions, WordN structures) stays with nassau-949.24.
