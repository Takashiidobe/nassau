# Implement INTEGER formatting and scanning

_2026-10-10 18:35:11_

Completed the INTEGER batch for Int, LargeInt, Position, FixedInt, and Int<N>. Added radix-aware Int.fmt and Int.scan, with Int.fromString delegated through StringCvt.scanString. Moved StringCvt before Int and removed Char's forward dependency on Int. LargeInt remains an Int alias pending arbitrary precision, already tracked by nassau-949.22; FixedInt and Int<N> are optional structures. No new runtime feature was identified.
