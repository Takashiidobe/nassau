# Directory listing and file queries in OS.FileSys

_2026-10-12 12:08:04_

OS.FileSys gains openDir, readDir, rewindDir, closeDir, isDir, fileSize, mkDir and rmDir through seven new EXTERN_FNS rows backed by std::fs in runtime/os.rs (directory streams share the handle table with files, so Prim.osClose closes both). wasi.js implements fd_readdir over the in-memory tree. tests/fixtures/basis/filesys.sml matches Poly/ML and also runs in browser-session.mjs. Still missing: access, modTime, getDir/chDir; see nassau-bda.
