# Cover Word8 families and finish Byte

_2026-10-24 09:19:38_

Work for nassau-949.14, left open because nassau-949.13 still blocks it. Word8Vector, Word8Array and their slices already existed as wrappers over generic Vector/Array, but no fixture used them and Byte only had byteToChar/charToByte. Added Byte.bytesToString, stringToBytes, unpackStringVec, unpackString and packString (raises Subscript out of range). byte.sml moved after mono-slice-impl.sml in basis/sources since it now depends on the Word8 structures. Added basis/word8-families.sml, checked against Poly/ML. A compact byte representation is not needed for Basis semantics and is not planned.
