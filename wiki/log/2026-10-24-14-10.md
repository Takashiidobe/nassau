# Fix browser basis load and gate the wasm profile

_2026-10-24 14:10:32_

The browser still failed after stubbing sock_accept/sock_shutdown: nassau_posix_int returned -ENOSYS for every op on non-unix targets, and posix.sml calls op 20 at load to fill Posix.Signal numbers, so the basis raised SysErr and BrowserRepl construction trapped. Hoisted the SIGNALS table out of the unix-only module in runtime/posix.rs (WASI uses Linux numbering) and answered op 20 in the non-unix stub. The wasm nextest profile already catches this class of drift (every fixture instantiates the runtime through wasi.js) but was not in the quality gates, so it was added to CLAUDE.md. Verified: ./www/build.sh then cargo nextest r --profile wasm --release, 233/233 pass.
