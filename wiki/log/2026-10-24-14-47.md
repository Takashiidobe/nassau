# Use the libc crate for the runtime's POSIX, time and stdio FFI

_2026-10-24 14:47:30_

Replaced the hand-written extern "C" declarations in runtime/posix.rs, os.rs, time.rs and nassau_runtime.rs with the libc crate (added as a dependency, locked at 0.2.189). Signal numbers, sysconf names, uname, times, tm and wait-status decoding now come from libc types and constants instead of per-OS tables. Kept small extern blocks for what libc lacks: ctermid, fegetround/fesetround and the stdout/stderr statics. wasi-libc has no signals, so non-unix targets keep the Linux numbers. rustix was checked and rejected: it omits fork, setuid/setgid, alarm, getlogin, ctermid, times and sysconf. Verified: clippy clean, wasm profile 233/233. The native nextest run was interrupted and not completed.
