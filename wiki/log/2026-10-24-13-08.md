# Native socket runtime

_2026-10-24 13:08:46_

Added TCP/Unix stream socket runtime (runtime/os.rs: create, pair, bind, listen, accept, connect, name, shutdown), Prim.sock* externs in src/core.rs and src/runtime.rs, and basis/socket.sml with Socket, INetSock (TCP), UnixSock and a minimal NetHostDB.
