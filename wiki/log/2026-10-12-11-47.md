# Share the Rust runtime with the browser and add OS I/O

_2026-10-12 11:47:41_

The browser now runs runtime/ built for wasm32-wasip1 (nassau_runtime.wasm, linked with --global-base=1114112 --no-stack-first so its data sits above the 1 MiB shadow stack) instead of the JS allocator and GC. All runtime exports take i64 words and gc.rs reads frames and fields as u64. www/frontend/wasi.js supplies WASI: stdout capture, exit, env, clock, random and an in-memory filesystem. MATH_FNS became EXTERN_FNS with typed params and results; one Prim::Extern covers libm and the new nassau_os_* calls in runtime/os.rs (open, read, write, close, remove, getenv, error messages). Basis gains OS.SysErr, OS.errorMsg, OS.FileSys.remove, OS.Process.getEnv and a real TextIO (file and standard streams). tests/fixtures/basis/textio-files.sml matches Poly/ML and also runs in browser-session.mjs. Follow-ups: nassau-6lt notes and two new beads.
