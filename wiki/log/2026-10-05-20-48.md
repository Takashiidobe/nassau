# Builtins 2b: real Word8, Posix.Process, OS.Process

_2026-10-05 20:48:30_

Posix.Process.exit and Word8.fromInt were parser special forms (StmtKind::Print/Exit, ExprKind::Word8FromInt/PosixExit, lower_statement rewrites) so fixtures could set exit codes. They are now prelude structures: Word8 (word = builtin type word8, fromInt wraps mod 256, toInt), Posix.Process.exit, and OS.Process (opaque status, success, failure, isSuccess, exit) over new primitives Prim.exit, Prim.word8OfInt and Prim.intOfWord8 (identity casts). word8 is now a nameable builtin type. The special forms are deleted from parser, infer, walk, lower and ast_dump, as is the lowering catch-all. The qualified-name builtin fallback in infer is restricted to Prim.*. val _ = e now always takes the general declaration path (one extra jump block in core dumps; regenerated core/top-level). New fixtures runtime/posix-exit, os-process, os-process-success (exit codes from Poly/ML).
