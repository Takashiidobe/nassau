# Cache the compiled Basis by content hash

_2026-10-11 20:30:48_

build.rs hashes basis/, src/ (minus repl.rs, main.rs, web.rs, ast_dump.rs), runtime/, build.rs, Cargo.toml and Cargo.lock, and reuses the compiled Basis from target/<profile>/nassau-bootstrap/basis-<target>/<key> on a match; the newest 8 keys are kept. Exclusion-based so new src files invalidate by default. Edits to repl.rs now rebuild in ~4.4s (no bootstrap), pipeline edits ~9s, reverts ~4.6s.
