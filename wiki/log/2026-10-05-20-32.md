# Builtins 1: length/map/foldl/o/before/ignore now SML

_2026-10-05 20:32:13_

Removed the Rust builtins length, map, foldl, o, before and ignore (infer entries, Builtin variants, list_length/list_function/compose IR, the infix o/before special cases). They are now SML: List.length/map/foldl in basis/list.sml and a new structure General (basis/general.sml) with top-level aliases. before is typed 'a * unit -> 'a by matching (). Cost: calls instead of inlined loops; behavior and stack use are unchanged (map still recurses non-tail). Fixture runtime/general-structure.sml.
