# Audit POSIX Basis runtime requirements

_2026-10-10 18:42:30_

Audited Posix, Posix.Error, Posix.FileSys, Posix.IO, and Posix.ProcEnv against the manual. No faithful pure-SML implementation is available for these interfaces: they require SysWord, host errno mappings, descriptors and filesystem calls, Word8 array/vector I/O, and process/environment services. Existing runtime beads track the missing support: nassau-617.16, .617.19, .617.20, and .617.22. Added them as dependencies of the optional Basis audit issue nassau-617.5. The existing Posix.Process.exit stub in basis/os.sml does not supply the documented Posix umbrella or these structures, and was left untouched.
